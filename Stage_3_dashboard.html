Methodology and limitations

Primary models S1–S4 are selected using the fixed Part D investor grid. S5 is retained in the Python engine as a solver-failure fallback. Dollar amounts are scaled from the $100,000 historical path under continuous weights and proportional fees; changing capital does not trigger new trades or a solver run.

Long-only relax-and-fix optimization, at most 150 liquid candidates, 10 bp proportional fees, duration tolerance 0.5 years, convexity tolerance 25 years², model-specific KRD bands, 1% post-trade cash cap and 2.5% terminal expected monthly tracking error cap. Restricted-solver optimality is not global cardinality optimality. Data-quality exclusions, redemption estimates and reconstructed risk exposures are limitations. No bond-lot constraints or live data. The 22-month calibration sample is not independently validated.

Research implementation: Stage_3_dashboard.py and the existing Stage 2 engine provide fresh optimization with the necessary Python packages and Gurobi license. Embedded holdings and risk-plan values are rounded for compactness; repository CSVs retain original precision.
